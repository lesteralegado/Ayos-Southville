# Ayos Southville pilot

An independent, resident-led reporting pilot. These terms distinguish coordinating a response from guaranteeing repairs or representing an official service.

## Language

**Controlled pilot**:
An invitation-based trial for 10-25 residents, with consent and release approval required before participation.
_Avoid_: Public rollout, official service

**Internal pilot desk**:
A resident-led point of responsibility for report review and follow-up, not an official barangay, utility or HOA partner.
_Avoid_: Government department, official recipient

**Report review**:
Acknowledgement of a concern and a decision about its next step, not a repair promise.
_Avoid_: Repair guarantee, resolution deadline

**External follow-up**:
Contact with an outside organization and a record of its response; forwarding alone does not establish acceptance or resolution.
_Avoid_: Official assignment, accepted referral

**Test scenario**:
A clearly labelled fictional exercise used to evaluate the report workflow, not evidence of an actual community concern, repair or adoption outcome.
_Avoid_: Actual resolution, real pilot finding
